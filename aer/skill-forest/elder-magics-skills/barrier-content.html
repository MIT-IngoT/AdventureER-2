Barrier - Bulwark I

Learn: 2 SP

Cost: X AP +1 UPKEEP

Effect: INSTEAD Create the Barrier on an adjacent Hex. It can be moved for 1 AP. It will absorb DMG passing through it and acts as Cover. Uses the standard AP spent / 2 (rounded down) formula for determining Hit Points.

Barrier - Bulwark I

Barrier - Bulwark II

Learn: 2 SP

Cost: X AP +1 UPKEEP

Effect: ALSO for every 1 Hit Points the Barrier has, it is 1 Hex longer. You may choose to shape/curve it when creating, and later rotate it instead of moving (still for 1 AP).

Barrier - Bulwark II

Effect: ALSO Range increases to 6 Hexes.

Barrier - Riposte II

Barrier - Riposte III

Learn: 2 SP

Cost: X AP

Effect: INSTEAD After absorbing the DMG, the Barrier strikes back for DMG equal to the total AP spent to initially create it (Range 6 Hexes).

Barrier - Riposte III

Barrier - Riposte I

Cost: X AP

Effect: ALSO You may expend extra AP when casting Barrier. After absorbing DMG, the Barrier strikes back for DMG equal to its remaining Hit Points (Range 4 Hexes).

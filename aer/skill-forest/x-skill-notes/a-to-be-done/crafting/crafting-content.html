  • Melee Weapon

    Xx Iron Ingot / X/2x Steel Ingot / Xx Gold Ingot / Xx Copper Ingot / 2Xx Scrap Metal

    • 4Xx Wood Bundle / X Plank / X Fabric
    • 1x Leather / 2x Bones / 2x Wood Bundle / 1x Scrap Metal / 1x Iron Ingot / 1x Steel Ingot / 1x Gold Ingot / 1x Copper Ingot

1st contribute 1:1, unlimited input.
2nd contribute 2:1, still with unlimited input.
3rd contribute 1:1, but are limited input.
=<5 Spaces = Short Weapon
6-9 Spaces = Medium Weapon
10+ Spaces = Heavy Weapon

Ex.: 3x Iron Ingots + 12x Wood Bundles + 1x Gold Ingot = 3 + 3 (6/2) + 1 = 7 Spaces = Medium Melee Weapon. OR 1x Steel Ingot + 1x Fabric + 2x Bones = 2 (1x2) + 1 + 2 = 5 Spaces = Short Melee Weapon. OR 12x Scrap Metal + 4 Plank + 1x Leather = 6 (12/2) + 4 + 1= 11 Spaces = Heavy Melee Weapon

T3 - Armoursmithing

  • Armour Piece

    Xx Iron Ingot / X/2x Steel Ingot / Copper Ingot / 2Xx Wood Bundle / 2Xx Leather / 2Xx Bones / 2Xx Scrap Metal

    • Xx Raw Mineral (Crystal) / X/2x Gold Ingot / Xx Copper Ingot
    • Y Fabric

1st contribute Physical Armour 1:1, unlimited input
2nd contribute Magical Armour 1:1, unlimited input
For every 3 materials spent, 1 Fabric is required (rounding down). Armour (item) Size is determined by dividing the sum of its final materials (including Fabric) by 3.

Ex.: 4x Iron Ingots + 12 Raw Mineral (Crystal) + 5 Fabric = 21. 21/3 = 7. The Armour has 4 Physical Pips, 12 Magical Pips, and takes up 7 inventory Spaces. OR 6x Steel Ingot + 4 Gold Ingot + 3 Fabric = 13. 13/3 = 4. The Armour has 12 Physical Pips, 8 Magical Pips, and takes up 4 inventory Spaces.

T3 - Alchemy

  • Healing Item

    2Xx Various Herbs / 2Xx Various Mushrooms / 2Xx Various Fruit / 4Xx Raw Meat

    • 2Xx Various Herbs / 2Xx Various Mushrooms / 2Xx Various Fruit / 4Xx Raw Meat
    • +2Xx Various Herbs / 2Xx Various Mushrooms / 2Xx Various Fruit / 4Xx Raw Meat
    • Container (Empty)

Various Herbs make the effect last 1 turn for every 2 Various Herbs used. Various Mushrooms make the healing effect increase by 1 every 2 Various Mushrooms used. Various Fruit increase the resulting dosage by 1 for every 2 Various Fruit used. Raw Meat can substitute any aforementioned ingredient at an increased cost.
The container used when crafting will be filled with the Healing item. Multiple Containers might be necessary for large dosages as one container can only hold 5 uses.

Ex.: 6x Various Herbs + 4x Various Mushrooms + 8x Raw Meat (Fruit substitute) + Empty Container = A healing effect that restores 2 HP every turn for 3 turns, and can be used 2 times. Fits in one Container.

Speaking of, Crafting skill finalised. It starts with one node then splits into two, and then one of the two splits into two again.

CONTAINERS ARE 1D4+1 SPACES LARGE

Level 1 - Crafting. You can make use of the basic crafting Kit/Station and learn all recepies associated with it.
Level 2A - Alchemy. You learn to make food items and The Healing Item. Also allows use of the Alchemy Kit/Station and recepies associated with it
Level 3A - Advanced Alchemy. You learn to make advanced consumables such as specific potions (that grant resistances), and Runes (more on that another time but basically item passives limited at one per)
Level 4A - SP SINK 5. Gain +1 Magical Armour Pip.
Level 2B - Smelting. You learn to use the Smelting Kit/Station and make ingots from ores you find.
Level 3B1 - Armoursmithing, you learn to use the Armour Smithy kit/station and the recepies associated with it
Level 4B1 - Armoursmithing. You gain +1 Physical Pip. SP SINK 5.
Level 3B2 - Weaponsmithing. You learn to use the anvil/weapon Smithy kit/station and make Drawn weapons (Longbow and Shortbow) as well as Arrows and Arrow Quivers, and melee weapons Level 4B2 - You learn to make mechanical weapons, their ammo and quivers/pouches to hold it

Echoes Base

Learn: 3 SP

Cost: 2 AP, UPKEEP 2.

Effect: Summon a spiritual companion that shares your HP pool. It can move independently within its operational range. You invest SP into its five attributes to customize its abilities, with each Tier costing Tier X SP.

You may also have it learn skills at a -1 SP discount, as skills do not transfer between the echo and the character summoning it. Echoes do not have INNATE skills, and cannot use equipment on their own. they may "learn" weapon attacks and related skills to use them as if they were skills.

Operational range refers to the distance you and your echo can be separated by. by default it is equal to your Speed +1.

Echo's base stats are shared / mirrored from yours (Vitals, Speed, Armour) you pay for echo actions using your AP unless stated otherwise.
